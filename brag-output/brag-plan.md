# Brag Plan: Branch

## What is this app?
A chat client where the conversation is a tree, not a line — ask a follow-up
about any phrase without derailing the main thread, branch to several models at
once, and pay less because branches share cached context.

## The angle
Every AI chat is a straight line. Ask about one word and the whole thread
follows you down the tangent. Branch makes the tangent its own coloured strand —
the main thread stays exactly where you left it. The video's job is to make that
one idea *land visually*: a straight thread, a phrase selected, a coloured branch
spurring off, then the graph blooming into a small tree — and the quiet kicker
that branches cost fewer tokens because they share cache.

Earnest, confident, not a joke. The product is genuinely clever; let the motion
be calm and let the branching visual do the bragging.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A single chat reply, dark UI. The phrase **"p value"** highlights, and the
**⑂ Branch** chip snaps in beside the cursor. One line under it:
**"Ask about any word — without derailing the thread."**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The branch spurs off.** The main thread holds; a blue **⑂ p value** strand
  curves out to the side. The point: the main line never moved.
- **The graph blooms.** Cut to the canvas: a grey main spine with three coloured
  branches (blue, amber, magenta) fanning out — one continuous strand each.
  "Every tangent is its own branch."
- **Ask three models at once.** One question fans into three labelled branches —
  **CLAUDE-OPUS-4-8 · GPT-5 · GEMMA3:4B** — each its own colour. "Compare models
  side by side."
- **The quiet kicker: it's cheaper.** The token meter **↑ · ↓** with a **cached**
  tag. "Branches share context. Caching does the rest."

## Outro / punchline
Wordmark on the dark cream-on-charcoal palette:
**"Branch — your conversation is a tree, not a line."**

## User flow worth showing
entry → key action → result, all real UI:
1. A reply on screen; select the phrase "p value" → the ⑂ Branch chip appears.
2. The branch opens as a coloured spur; the main thread stays put.
3. The Graph tab: main spine + coloured branch strands; then one question fans
   out to three model-labelled branches.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet, confident product film for a thinking tool
- Interpretation: few scenes, longer holds, restrained motion. Confidence through
  calm. The colour comes only from the branch strands — everything else is the
  app's warm-neutral charcoal. No hype words, no bouncing.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm professional bed with sparse, motion-matched accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM, warm/corporate) — restrained
- Music treatment: enter ~0.3s, hold at a low bed volume (~0.5), gentle fade
  under the final wordmark
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset present (120.19 BPM). Strong cues cluster
  17.02 / 17.52 / 18.52 / 20.02s — target the model-fan-out payoff and the final
  wordmark landing near those. Beat grid ~0.5s apart from 3.02s for small accents.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the graph's branch glow / card presence
  breathe with music RMS. No bars, no strobes.
- SFX posture: sparse. A soft key-tick when the branch chip snaps in; a light
  card sound as each of the three model branches arrives; one quiet announcement
  swell on the wordmark. Nothing busy.
- Audio-coupled moments: the ⑂ Branch chip snap; the three model branches arrive
  one by one (card sounds); the final wordmark.
- Restraint rule: audio must never get loud or comedic. This is a calm film.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: select & branch — 4.5s
Dark chat UI, one assistant reply visible. Cursor selects the phrase "p value";
the text highlights and the **⑂ Branch** chip snaps in. Line settles beneath:
**"Ask about any word — without derailing the thread."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate the text selection, then the ⑂ chip snaps in.
Audio intent: quiet confidence; a single soft key-tick on the chip snap.
Audio-coupled idea: ⑂ chip snap = one subtle interface tick.
Music: warm bed enters low.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The branch spurs off — 3.5s
The main thread stays fixed; a blue **⑂ p value** branch curves out to the right
with its own short reply. Caption: **"The main thread never moved."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — the branch strand draws out from the reply.
Audio intent: a gentle rise; the branch "arrives".
Audio-coupled idea: soft card/whoosh as the branch strand completes.
Music: bed continues.
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The graph blooms — 4s
Cut to the Graph canvas: grey main spine, three coloured branch strands (blue,
amber, magenta) fanning down and to the right, each one continuous.
Caption: **"Every tangent is its own branch."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — the three strands draw/settle, slightly staggered.
Audio intent: the "reveal" — the shape of the idea.
Audio-coupled idea: three light ticks as strands settle (every-other-beat).
Music: bed swells slightly toward the next scene.
Transition mood: clean cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Ask three models at once — 4.5s
One question card at top; three labelled branches fan out below —
**CLAUDE-OPUS-4-8** (blue), **GPT-5** (amber), **GEMMA3:4B** (magenta) — each
arriving one by one. Caption: **"Ask three models at once. Compare."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — the three model branches arrive one by one, each
with a card sound; hold the full set on screen ~1.2s afterward so all three read.
Audio intent: the payoff; three matched card sounds landing near strong cues.
Audio-coupled idea: card sound per model branch; beat-lock the third to a strong cue.
Music: strong cues ~17–18.5s — land the third branch here.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Cheaper by design + wordmark — 3.5s
Brief token meter beat: **↑ 640 · ↓ 160 · cached** with a one-line caption
**"Branches share context. Caching does the rest."** — then it resolves into the
wordmark: **Branch — your conversation is a tree, not a line.**
Sequential/interaction: the meter reads, then the wordmark lands.
Audio intent: one quiet announcement swell on the wordmark, music fades under it.
Audio-coupled idea: wordmark landing near the 20.02s strong cue; music fade-out.
Music: gentle fade under the wordmark.
Transition mood: settle / fade out (end)

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat-but-restrained warm corporate bed.
**Audio summary:** a low warm bed throughout, three matched card accents on the
model fan-out landing near the strong cues, one soft announcement swell on the
wordmark as the music fades — calm, professional, never busy.
